The Comeback of the Big Screen

For years after the pandemic, movie theaters have been suffering from lack of activity. Now, theaters are seeing renewed ticket prices, and films that did better in the box office also tended to sell more theater tickets. Young audiences are beginnning to value the collective experience of film once again.
